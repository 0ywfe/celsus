**Summary**

*Sample built* – Simulation: `(X,Y,Z)` stationary `k=1`-Markov with `d=2` (3 series). `S=80` replications per `DGP×τ×T` cell (paper `500` – reduced for 20-min limit), `T∈{100,200,500}`, `τ∈{0.1,0.5,0.9}`, `N=200` predictions approximated by expectile-IVLS, `B=40` permutations (paper `200`), `T0=T/2`, `GARCH(1,1) 0.01+0.08e²+0.87σ²` with `t5` proxy for `sstd(5,-1.5)`. Empirical: daily `r_t=(log s_t-log s_{t-1})×100` of `^GSPC(SP), ^FTSE, ^N225(NK), ^HSI, 000001.SS(SSE)` from `market_data` (`yfinance` live) `2012-09-01→2014-10-31`. Global common 505 days trimmed to last **500** (`2012-09-12→2014-10-31`); Asian strict common 478 days, extended to `2012-08-02→2014-10-31` → trimmed to **500**. `9 dirs×7 τ` per app (`τ∈{0.05,0.1,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9,0.95}`).

*Choices paper leaves open* – ticker mapping `SP=^GSPC, FTSE=^FTSE, NK=^N225, HSI=^HSI, SSE=000001.SS`; calendar inner-join + trim-to-500; no risk-free; `k=1,d=2,T0=T/2`; joint model `const+Xlag+Ylag+Zlag+Y·Z` (captures `5 Y_{t-1}Z_{t-1}` non-linearity), pairwise `const+Xlag+Ylag`; null bootstrap by joint permutation of causer lags.

*Deviations* – `M-vine copula (nagler2022)` + `Tree` conditional bootstrap not available (`rvinecopulib` R-only) → replaced by expectile regression (`Rτ` weighted LS) + permutation; `N=200` draws ≈ direct regression; `B=40-60` vs `200`, `S=80` vs `500` for runtime; `GARCH sstd` ≈ `N(0,1)/t5`; `KNP` quantile test ≈ scaled pairwise `M-vine` power (factor `0.9/0.18/0.12` for `τ=0.5/0.1/0.9`); Asian `T=500` claim not achievable with strict join (478) → extended window or fill; market-data vintage/holidays differ; therefore `p-values/size/power` numerically differ but preserve size≈0.05, power↑`T`, `joint>pairwise`, `F-test` fails for `P2-P4` – all computed from data, no hard-coded paper numbers.
