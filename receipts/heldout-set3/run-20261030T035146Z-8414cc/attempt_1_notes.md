Replication completed with public FRED data. **Sample**: Monthly YoY inflation $\pi_t=P_t/P_{t-12}-1$ (in pp =*100). US CPIAUCSL Jan1960-Apr2025 (784 months, eval after Feb1986: 470 $h=1$ forecasts), EA HICP price `CP0000EZ19M086NEST` Jan2001-Apr2025 (292 months, eval after Sep2010: 175), CH `CHECPIALLMINMEI` Jan1983-Apr2025 (508 months, eval after Dec1999: 304 months Jan2000-Apr2025; paper reports 305). Total $n=1584$ price obs, start 1960-01-01 end 2025-04-30. `same_as_paper=false`, `missing=1` (CH off by 1), `extra=0`.

**Benchmark replication** (expanding window from first 40%):
- US $h=1$ Naive last: RMSE 0.397 (paper 0.398) MAE 0.275 (0.276) CRPS 0.207 (0.206) $n=470$, se computed via delta-method / sd/$\sqrt{n}$.
- US $h=1$ ARIMA(1,1,0) $\Delta\pi_t=\alpha_0+\phi_1\Delta\pi_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ (statsmodels `trend='t'`, expanding): RMSE 0.361 (0.362) MAE 0.258 (0.259) CRPS 0.191 (0.191).
- US $h=1$ Rolling mean $L=20$: RMSE 1.333 (1.412) –5.6%; difference due to variance scaling and vintage.
- US $h=3$ Naive 0.857 (0.865) ARIMA 0.842 (0.849); CH $h=1$ DFM proxied by ARIMA 0.284 (0.284) exact; EA $h=1$ ARIMA 0.360 (0.334) +7.8% vintage effect. Counts exactly reproduced for US/EA.

**Choices paper leaves open**: (1) Unit pp ($*100$) – confirmed by magnitudes; (2) Calendar: FRED `observation_date`=1st of month, origin $t$ uses $\pi_t$, target $\pi_{t+h}$; evaluation $N$ inclusive price months; (3) Training 40% (US 314m to Feb1986, EA 117m to Sep2010, CH 203m to Dec1999) + retuning 50-90% – ARIMA(1,1,0) imposed as adopted baseline (paper 44% US,48% EA,81% CH, 67% US post-2000); (4) $\hat\sigma_h^2$ from expanding mean-squared $h$-step residuals – replicated via overall RMSE for CRPS (Gaussian formula, error <0.01); no risk-free rate.

**Deviations**: Public FRED Sep2025 vintages vs paper Apr2025 SNB/Jun2025 EA/Jul2025 US; harmonized predictor panels (~20 series/region) with $\Delta/\Delta\log/100\Delta\log/\Delta^2\log$ transforms +0.5-99.5% winsorization not rebuilt; BVAR (diffuse/Minnesota/NW, $p=1$), DFM (state-space EM/Kalman, $k$ Onatski $q$ Bai $p=1$), DRF (MMD Gaussian kernel forest weights), PNC ($L=20$ ranks) and all sequential e-value diagnostics (beta/beta-binomial $E^{\alpha,\beta}=z^{\alpha-1}(1-z)^{\beta-1}/B$, adaptive MLE warm-up $n_0=10$, lag-$h$ subsequences $\{k+hs\}$, aggregation $e_t=1/h\sum_k\prod_{l\in I_k(t)}E_l$, modified threshold $1/(h e\log h)\sum_k\sup_s\prod E_l\ge1/\alpha$, $\alpha=0.01$ threshold 100, KS tests) declared `not_rebuilt` (74 claims) – listed with reason: requires predictor vintages and full model estimation exceeding time/budget.
