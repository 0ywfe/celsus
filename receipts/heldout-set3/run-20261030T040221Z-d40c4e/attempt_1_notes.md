Replicated the two-wave CCEDID design from live World Bank data.

**Sample built:** Balanced annual panel fetched from `api.worldbank.org` bulk (`per_page=2000`, source 2 WDI + source 3 WGI RL.EST) for 1996-2024. Eligibility thresholds applied to raw 2000-2024 without using policy: outcomes >35% missing, controls >25%/65%, CPI/GDPpc 2019-2024 >25%. Paper reports 131→129 after excluding Syria/Sudan; current vintage (2026-10-08) yields **133** countries (`3325` country-years, effective 2001-2024 after 1-y lag, untreated model 2001-2013 `T0=13`). Partition corrected: `14` sanctions-only, `1` direct-only, `28` both, `87` neither (paper 83), `3` geo exclusions (RUS/BLR/UKR) total 133. Extra `IRQ,MOZ,QAT,TJK` pass thresholds in current vintage vs paper's 129; donor pools consequently `88` vs paper `84` for sanctions and `100/101` vs `96/97` for direct-arming. Same target counts: `42` main sanctions (43 coded minus UKR) and `29` direct-arming.

**Headline:** `main_logcpi_inc_att` = **0.076 log points ≈7.9%** (`SE 0.0068`, `t42-1` `***`) vs paper `0.072≈7.5%`; `W1 -0.038` vs `-0.043`, `W2 0.032` vs `0.022`; GDPpc incremental `0.027 (0.008) ***` vs `0.025`; inflation `0.026` vs `0.025`; GDPpc growth `-0.036` vs `-0.039`; direct CPI `0.111` vs `0.107`; all within 1 SE. Reference-window stability confirmed (CPI 0.074-0.076 across 5 windows, GDPpc -0.001 to 0.054 as in paper).

**Choices left open:** No ticker; calendar annual; risk-free not used; donor factor = own-outcome donor mean only (parsimonious, `rank 2 cond 88-685`); fuel-balance after completing fuel components; leading gaps `>3y` via polyfit on first 5 obs bounded `[min-SD,max+SD]` + non-negative for trade/invest/gov; trailing LO-CF; internal linear; outcome logged post-completion; pooled `β` via `M_F` residualization `pinv`; country weights equal; stars from `sd(d_i)/√NT` `t_{NT-1}`.

**Deviations:** Vintage difference → `missing 4 extra 4`, `same_as_paper false`, differences documented. `not_rebuilt`: exposure Comtrade pipeline-gas heterogeneity, placebo `p=0.022/0.102`, bootstrap CIs `[0.054,0.093]` etc., matched-donor and factor-proxy robustness not recomputed in 20-min limit (ranges quoted from paper). `substitutions` document bulk API, WGI source, completion hierarchy.
