Replication built from public data: simulated DGPs S1,S2,P1-P4 (k=1 Markov, d=2) and Yahoo Finance indices ^GSPC, ^FTSE, ^N225, ^HSI, 000001.SS (Shanghai Composite). Daily log-returns r_t = (log s_t - log s_{t-1})*100 over Sept 2012-Oct 2014. Common trading-day inner join gives 506 bars (505 returns) for SP/FTSE/NK trimmed to 500 and 479 bars (478 returns) for NK/HSI/SSE; paper claims 500 for both.

**Choices left open:** ticker mapping as above (standard Yahoo); calendar alignment via inner join; no risk-free rate needed; M-vine selection/estimation (Nagler 2022) not publicly replicable; KNP quantile test not replicable; skewed-t (sstd ν=5, ξ=-1.5) not uniquely defined; T0=T/2 exactly; N=200, B=200, α=0.05, k=1, d=2 as stated.

**Deviations:** M-vine copula test replaced by expectile regression (asymmetric least squares) with Y*Z interaction proxy for vine dependence; GARCH sstd approximated by Gaussian GARCH; KNP approximated by linear F pairwise; bootstrap p-values via residual resampling under restricted model instead of vine-tree sampling; simulation replications 300-400 (vs 500) for speed; Asian T=478 not 500; empirical p-values differ numerically (e.g., global FTSE→SP tail pattern inverted) but headline joint left-tail causality (NK,SSE)→HSI at τ=0.05 is reproduced (p=0.005 vs paper 0.005, strongly significant vs pairwise NK→HSI 0.010 and SSE→HSI 0.105). Size control and power monotonicity with T preserved qualitatively.
